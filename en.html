Mochi in your pocket

Your day on the iPhone

Habits, tasks and deadlines on one page. It works on its own, with no Mac and no account.

Linked to your Mac

A code or a QR code, and your whole notebook follows you: what you tick, create or change on one side reaches the other within seconds. Encrypted with your code, and it doesn't use your iCloud storage.

Reminders at your time

"Read 20 pages" at 9 pm, only on days it's planned and only if it isn't ticked yet.

Free, then one purchase

The free version keeps 3 habits, 3 tasks and 3 deadlines. Try everything for 14 days; after that, a single purchase, no subscription. Mochi for Mac stays free.

Studies

Your studies, finally in order

It understands your deadlines

In-class exams, work to hand in, personal projects: Mochi works out the type and files each one under the right course, abbreviations included.

Paste your list

All your dates at once, the way you wrote them down. Mochi guesses the type and the course and spots dates already past; you check before adding.

Revision where it's missing

One button suggests sessions for every exam or assignment that doesn't have enough, in your free moments, never on top of a class.

The study timeline

The coming weeks at a glance: classes and holidays, the preparation for each exam or assignment, your revision sessions.

Together

Habits with friends

At your pace

Cleaning twice a month, the dentist twice a year: the habit comes back in Today at the right moment, then waits for the next month or year.

Share your habits

One or several, with one or several friends. A friend shares a habit you don't have? "Add" brings it over in one click, with the same rhythm.

See how they're doing

Next to the habit in Today, and day by day under it in The month. When a friend ticks, their character comes to tell you on your Dock.

With a simple code

"Create a code", send it, your friend pastes it into "Join". Everything is encrypted with the code and goes through Apple's servers without using iCloud storage: neither Apple nor anyone else can read your data.

Your coach

It listens, and it remembers

Good morning, here's your day

Each time you open your Mac, Mochi hops up and tells you in a bubble what's ahead: the next class and its room, your tasks, today's habits.

A real coach for your goals

It asks its questions, prepares the full plan up to the date, rest days included, and adjusts it after each session based on how you felt.

Your preferences

Right-click a slot: "on weekdays I don't like running at lunchtime". Mochi remembers and won't plan anything there again.

Draw your own

Trace one outline and pick a colour. Mochi rounds the shape, adds the eyes and fits it to wear accessories.

What it does

A notebook that tidies itself

Habits

Every day, some days, three times a week, twice a month or once a year. A monthly grid, like a bullet journal.

Mornings

When you open your Mac: the weather, your first class and its room, exams coming up. Then mood, energy, motivation.

Studies

Your timetable imported from a PDF, exams, assignments, applications, a timeline of the weeks ahead and a focus timer per subject.

Budget

Drop in your bank statement: expenses are sorted, and the charts show where your money goes.

⌥ Space

From any app: "revise maths Thursday 6 pm". Done. Press twice to dictate.

Time capsule

A letter to yourself, sealed until the day you choose: after exams, in six months, in a year.

The assistant

It talks, if you want it to

Mochi works entirely without AI. To talk to it ("organise my week", "sort this statement"), connect the service you prefer: a free NVIDIA key, Claude, OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, Gemini, or an AI running on your Mac with LM Studio or Ollama.

Everything stays on your Mac. Only your request to the assistant goes to the service you chose.

Install

Thirty seconds

  1. Download Mochi.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Mochi into the Applications folder.
  3. Open it from Launchpad: the character appears on your Dock.

Mochi switches to English on its own if your Mac is in English (or in Settings). Received it through WhatsApp and macOS refuses to open it? Download it here instead. Otherwise, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Mochi.app
